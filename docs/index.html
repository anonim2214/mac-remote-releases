MacRemote

Your iPhone as a trackpad and keyboard for your Mac.

Move the pointer, type, run shortcuts, change the volume, open Mission Control and put the Mac to sleep — over your own Wi-Fi, encrypted, with no server in between.

Download the Mac app

macOS 14 or later · free · signed and notarised by Apple

How it works

  • Install the small Mac app above. It lives in the menu bar.
  • Grant Accessibility when it asks — macOS requires it before any app may move the pointer or type.
  • Open MacRemote on your iPhone, tap your Mac, enter the six-digit code it shows. Once.
