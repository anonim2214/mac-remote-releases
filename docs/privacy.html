Privacy Policy

MacRemote · last updated 23 September 2026

MacRemote collects nothing. There is no account to create, no sign-up, no analytics, and no server of ours anywhere in the picture.

What leaves your devices

Nothing. The iPhone app talks to the companion app on your Mac and to nothing else. Both have to be on the same local network, and they find each other over Bonjour without asking anyone.

What travels between the two

While you are controlling your Mac, the phone sends what you do — pointer movement, taps, typed text, key combinations, volume and brightness changes — and the Mac sends back what the phone needs to show you: its name, battery level, what is playing, and the icons of the apps in your Dock. This traffic is encrypted, and the two devices authenticate each other with a key established when you paired them.

What is stored, and where

The phone remembers which Mac it last used and its pairing key. The Mac remembers the devices you have paired with it. Both live in the ordinary settings storage of each app, on the device itself. Deleting the app removes them.

Permissions the Mac app asks for

macOS requires the Accessibility permission before any application may move the pointer or type. MacRemote uses it for exactly that, and for reading which app is in front so the phone can show you the right thing. It is never used to read the contents of your screen or your documents.

Children

MacRemote is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone.
